# Med Term Wing

A Wing Commander style space combat game for learning medical terminology. Fly from the carrier Lexicon, lock on to enemy fighters, and answer the targeting computer to fire. Break shielded gunships by building terms one word part at a time.

**Play it:** https://kashee12345.github.io/med-term-wing/

Created by Dr. Akash Garg.

## What is in it

- 12 sectors, one for each body system: Word Parts, Body Structure, Integumentary, Musculoskeletal, Cardiovascular, Blood and Immune, Respiratory, Digestive, Urinary, Nervous, Endocrine and Reproductive
- 180 targeting questions and 48 word-building chains, each with an explanation
- 6 mission types: Dogfight, Nav Patrol, Asteroid Field, Word Run, Escort and Capital Assault
- A walkable carrier with a briefing room, hangar, ready room with a kill board, and a med bay that reviews every term you missed
- Voiced wingmen who give hints, a throttle and brake, and an autopilot to nav points

## Controls

- Arrows, WASD or mouse to steer, Space to fire
- R or + faster, V or minus slower, X to brake, Shift for afterburner
- F to track your target, T to switch targets, H to ask a wingman for a hint
- C cockpit view, N autopilot to the next nav point, M sound
- On the carrier: WASD or arrows to walk, drag to look, E to use

Progress is saved in your browser. The whole game is one file, `index.html`, and runs offline once loaded.
